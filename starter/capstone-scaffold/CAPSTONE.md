# Capstone (Days 14-15): a small multi-agent system, built on your own

The capstone **extends your own L8-L13 work** to a new scenario. Pick one scenario in `scenarios/` (or agree your own at the Day-12 scoping session).

## Minimum requirements (all of them)
- [ ] At least **three agent roles**, one of them an **independent reviewer with its own context**
- [ ] Tools with at least **one guarded action**
- [ ] A **knowledge source with citations** and a **role-based access check**
- [ ] Hand-offs **checked against schemas**, **saved state**, and an **approval step** for risky actions
- [ ] An **eval of at least 15 cases** with a **held-out group**, a **threshold script**, and **one measured improvement**
- [ ] A **trace** and a short **cost report**
- [ ] A **threat model** with **two attacks tried and fixed**
- [ ] **Process evidence**: a spec, a plan, review findings and a decision log

## Timeline
- Day 12 (1 h): scoping session. Choose scenario, plan your minimum scope.
- Day 14: 10-minute one-to-one design check with a facilitator (10:00-12:00); eval and trace review at 13:30.
- Day 15: finish line (final eval run, threat model, cost report), 7-minute demo + questions, retrospective and 30/60/90-day adoption plan.

## Submit
Repository, eval report, cost report, threat model, decision log (see `submission_checklist.md`).
