# Postmortem scoring rubric (L12)
| Dimension | What is checked | Points |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Root cause matches the logs; times match the logs; planted mistakes in chat/timeline are NOT repeated | 5 |
| Completeness | title, summary, root cause, timeline, impact, action items all present | 3 |
| Cost | tokens (and so $) vs the single-agent baseline | 1 |
| Time | seconds vs the single-agent baseline | 1 |
Compare single vs team on all four, and write when the team was and was not worth it in `comparison.md`.
