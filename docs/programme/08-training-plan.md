## Training plan

How each day runs, and how to keep a group of beginners moving together.

### Daily rhythm

| Time | Block | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 09:30 | Check-in and recap (15 min) | One question from yesterday, and one participant explains something they built. |
| 09:45 | Concepts (about 105 min) | Short blocks of 20–30 minutes with a quick question or poll after each. |
| 11:30 | Guided walkthrough (45 min) | The facilitator live-codes the first steps of the day's lab while you follow along. |
| 12:15 | Lunch (1 h) |  |
| 13:15 | Lab, working on your own (3 h) | Facilitators circulate. Hint cards and cheat sheets are available. Office hour for the last 30 minutes. |
| 16:15 | Debrief (30 min) | Two participants show results, you explain one thing back, and the facilitator names the one lesson of the day. |
| 16:45 | Close | A 15-minute pre-read for tomorrow at most. |

#### Keeping everyone together

- Everyone starts from the same scaffolds; they get thinner as the course goes on.
- The guided walkthrough means nobody starts a lab cold.
- Daily office hour in the last 30 minutes of the lab block.
- Recorded walkthroughs and reference solutions for catching up.
- Everyone explains one thing back at each debrief.

#### Cohort and facilitation

- Up to 18 participants per cohort.
- One facilitator per six participants.
- Extra environment support on Days 1–2 and Day 6.
- Run the cohort in person or live-online; labs are the same either way.
