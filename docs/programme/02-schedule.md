## Schedule at a glance

Fifteen working days in three weeks. Select a day for its full syllabus.

Week 1

### Foundations and AI-native development

Learn how models and agents behave, then work **with** an agent: specs, tests, review, reusable assets.

**1**How LLMs behave, and how to talk to them**2**Context engineering and the anatomy of an agent**3**Spec-driven development**4**Verification, independent review and reusable agent assets**5**Agents across the SDLC, parallel work, Week-1 checkpoint

Week 2

### Building single-agent applications

Build an agent step by step: tools, limits, knowledge (RAG), evaluation, tracing and cost.

**6**Tool design and MCP**7**Single-agent design: planning, recovery, guardrails**8**Knowledge and memory: RAG**9**Evaluation: the unit tests of agents**10**Observability, cost and reliability, Week-2 checkpoint

Week 3

### Multi-agent systems, security and capstone

Combine agents, make them safe and recoverable, then prove it in an individual capstone.

**11**Multi-agent patterns**12**State, orchestration and human-in-the-loop**13**Security, safety and governance for agents**14**Capstone build day**15**Capstone finish, demos and assessment
