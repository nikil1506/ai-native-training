## The stuck ladder

1. Re-read the task and the hint card (5 min).
2. Ask your coding assistant to **explain** the error, not to fix it.
3. Check this cheat sheet.
4. Ask a facilitator.
5. Skip to the next task, mark it, and catch up in office hours.
